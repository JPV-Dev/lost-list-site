About Lost List

An Impossible Task.
Made Reality by AI.

After a disaster, creating a personal property list is often the most daunting step in recovery. We turn that impossible task into a comprehensive reality.

The Problem

After a wildfire, flood, or disaster, insurance companies require detailed personal property inventories. Where do you even begin? Families are left trying to remember every item they owned — while still processing the loss of their home or business.

Our Solution

Lost List's AI analyzes your home profile — property type, rooms, lifestyle, household composition — and generates a comprehensive inventory using machine learning models trained on real household data.

The Result

A complete, professional-grade inventory document ready for your insurance company. Every room covered, every item accounted for, every detail documented — in a fraction of the time it would take manually.
