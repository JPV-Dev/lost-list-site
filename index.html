About

Rebuilding Starts with
Knowing What You Had

When disaster strikes, the last thing you should worry about is remembering every item you owned.

Lost List helps homeowners and insurance professionals build comprehensive, room-by-room inventories of personal property. Whether you're recovering from a fire, flood, or other loss, we use AI-powered tools and expert guidance to ensure nothing gets overlooked.

The result: accurate claim documentation that leads to faster, fairer settlements — so you can focus on what matters most.
